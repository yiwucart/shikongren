## What this site is

This is a **free public archive** of articles by Lü Wenyi, author of the WeChat public account “Shikongren Coordinates” (时空坐标) and founder of the Lü School of Shikongren (吕氏时空人).

The purpose is simple: to keep these articles intact, and to make them findable and readable by people who would otherwise never encounter them. **No charge, no course sales, no commercial use of any kind.**

## Where the content comes from

- Every article comes from the WeChat public account “**Shikongren Coordinates**” and is attributed to **Lü Wenyi**.
- Copyright remains with the author and the publishing platform. This site has no commercial relationship with the author.
- Each article carries its source, author and a link to the Chinese original.
- Only publicly published articles are included. No student information is archived.

## A note on the translations

The English section is a **human translation, not machine output**. These articles concern Chinese metaphysical arts — Bazi (八字), feng shui (风水), Liuyao (六爻) — whose technical vocabulary has no settled English equivalents. A literal rendering produces sentences that are grammatically English and conceptually empty.

Our approach is therefore two-layered:

- **Keep the term.** Pinyin on first use — *tiangan dizhi*, *yongshen*, *tiaohou* — so the term can be checked against the Chinese original and against the wider literature.
- **Explain the function.** What the term actually does in the system, in plain English, before it is used in an argument.

A glossary of core terms accompanies the translations for this reason. Where the author is arguing rather than describing — and he usually is — the argument is carried over intact, including the passages that attack his own tradition. Nothing has been softened to make the material more palatable to a Western reader. Where a passage has been condensed, this is stated in a translator's note at the head of the article.

**The Chinese original remains the authoritative text.**

## About the Lü School of Shikongren

The Lü School of Shikongren is a system of prediction and feng shui founded by Lü Wenyi. Its stated premise is **science, logic and observed fact**, and it approaches traditional prediction through the relation between person, time and space — drawing a hard line between folk, psychological and entertainment readings on one side, and natural-scientific reasoning that can be verified, repeated and falsified on the other.

Its principal components include:

- **Lü School Natural Science Feng Shui** — form, qi-principle and numerical method cross-checked against one another, replacing single-formula judgement.
- **Lü School Natural Science Bazi** — fate graded by “manifest energy / latent energy,” with improvement sought simultaneously across time, space and behaviour.
- **300 Unconditional Judgements** — maxims verified repeatedly over thirty years of practice that carry no conditions of use.
- **The 290 Configurations, the 24 Returning-Soul Configurations, and the Three Circulations** — advanced technical series.
- **Date selection, Liuyao, Qimen, Meihua Yishu, Shenbing Qimen, physiognomy, character analysis, dream interpretation, stock analysis** and some ten other specialisms.

The author's own stated distinction between this system and most of what circulates as “metaphysics” is whether an inference can be made **logically coherent and repeatedly tested against fact**, rather than resting on intuition, experience or associative imagery.

## About the author

Lü Wenyi (吕文艺) is the founder of the Lü School of Shikongren. He holds a diploma in geography, a bachelor's degree in Chinese literature and an MBA, and lived for many years in Beijing and Hong Kong.

For the eight years before 2015 he was invited to teach a comparative course — “Natural Science Feng Shui versus Traditional Humanist Feng Shui” — on the Yixue and Architectural Feng Shui master's and doctoral programme at Beijing Normal University.

For more than thirty years he has held to three principles in his work — **science, logic and objective fact** — and has taught publicly at no charge for most of that time.
