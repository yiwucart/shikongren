> **Translator's note.** One chart, two twins, one exam, and a prediction made before the results: the shorter brother will score higher. The mechanism is stated so plainly it can be checked — the month stem *geng* metal is an unfavourable god, and if it genuinely supports the day master the boy grows tall and his fate platform drops; the hour stem *ren* water is the useful god, and the more it drains the day master the shorter he grows and the better he does. Height is the base platform, and everything else in this system is read off it.

**Male: Xinsi, Gengyin, Gengxu, Renwu (twins)**

**Luck cycle: Wuzi, from 2015. Annual cycle: Jihai.**

The problem of predicting the differing fates of twins has, in three thousand years, been solved thoroughly by no school of bazi except the Lü Wen-yi bazi model theory.

Analysis of this chart:

*Geng* is the day master, and the day master is height. If the day master *geng* genuinely receives the support of the month stem *geng*, and if the hour stem *ren* water does not genuinely perform its draining function, then a person born under this chart must be tall — at least 1.75 metres — because in the original chart metal is the unfavourable god; the day master *geng* metal can grow tall only because the unfavourable month stem *geng* supports it. Therefore a tall person born under this chart has, overall, a lower fate level than a short one.

If the month stem *geng* does not genuinely support the day stem *geng*, and *ren* water fully drains the day stem *geng*, then the *geng* day master will be short. Because the month stem *geng* is the unfavourable god, and an unfavourable god does not help the day master *geng*, while the hour stem *ren* water is the useful god and throws all its strength into helping the day stem *geng* — therefore, among people born under this chart, the short ones have a better overall fate platform than the tall ones.

At the university entrance examination: the hour stem *ren* water is the eating-and-wounding, and only the person under this chart in whom *ren* water functions more will do well in the examination. So it follows that **of the twins, the shorter one will score better than the taller one.**

The facts: the elder brother is 1.72 metres and 70 kilograms; the younger is 1.80 metres and 78 kilograms. In the 2019 entrance examination the elder scored 530 and the younger 460.

Objectively: among all boys in the country born under this chart, in this year's entrance examination, the shorter scored better and the taller scored worse. Because height is the base platform of the differing fates of people born under the same bazi; on that platform, because each person's various body features differ, the same stem or branch in the same chart does good or harm to each person with different strength — and so the great divergence of fates among people born under one chart comes about.

Study bazi and you will see: any school of bazi that cannot face the problem of predicting the differing fates of people born under the same chart and of the same sex, and cannot solve it completely, is congenitally disabled, and does not possess the objective truth needed to guide a human life.
