> **Translator's note.** The working manual for reading a single year. Three mechanisms, in order: chaotic activation of a ten god, the storage vaults and what may enter them, and the eight ways a useful and an unfavourable god can interact across a year. The vault rules are the part worth slowing down for — Lü derives them from soil behaviour (mud, ore, frozen gravel, farmland) rather than from the classical tables, and says outright that the traditional rules fail in practice. Seven worked cases follow, with the actual outcome after each reading.

## 1. The problem of ten gods in chaotic motion

In annual-cycle prediction, when four or more stems and branches of the same element are activated, that element's ten god is treated as being in chaotic motion.

Chaotic motion is, by definition, inauspicious. Which *kind* of misfortune it is depends on what that ten god represents.

**1. The wealth star in chaotic motion**

(1) Loss of money.

(2) For a man, the wealth star stands for wife and lover. In chaotic motion, marriage and emotional life are unstable and damaged.

(3) For a small child, the wealth star means being reunited with family and getting the toy he has longed for. In chaotic motion, separation from family, loss of the beloved toy.

(4) At specific positions, illness of particular organs. (Note: any ten god can stand for illness.)

**2. The officer-and-killer in chaotic motion**

(1) Injury, illness, disaster.

(2) Deterioration of the job, removal from post, arrest.

**3. The parallel-and-rob in chaotic motion**

(1) Money taken from you.

(2) Disputes and trouble.

**4. The owl-seal in chaotic motion**

(1) Running into troublesome matters.

(2) Losing or selling a car; damage to the house, or selling it.

(3) The company does not fold, but moves premises.

**5. The eating-and-wounding in chaotic motion**

(1) Reckless investment fails, money is lost, costs cannot be recovered quickly.

(2) Inviting lawsuits and disputes.

(3) Disaster to children. Not necessarily injury or illness — it may be a long separation from them.

## 2. The storage vaults

1. *Chen* is the water vault. It can take in *ren, gui, hai, zi* (water) and also *chou, wei* (earth).
2. *Xu* is the fire vault. It can take in *bing, ding, si, wu* (fire) and also *chou, wei* (earth).
3. *Chou* is the metal vault. It can take in *geng, xin, shen, you* (metal).
4. *Wei* is the wood vault. It can take in *jia, yi, yin, mao* (wood).

**Note: *chou* and *wei* earth do not take each other into storage, but each can enter the *chen* earth vault and the *xu* earth vault. *Chen* and *xu* do not take each other into storage, nor do they enter any other vault.**

**Wu and ji earth enter the *chen* and *xu* vaults; they cannot enter the *chou* and *wei* vaults.**

Why is it read this way?

In plain terms: *chen* earth is thin mud; *xu* earth is ore and rock; *chou* earth is frozen ground and gravel; *wei* earth is good farmland. Traditional fate studies hold that *xu* earth does not generate metal. That does not match the facts and does not survive practice. All earth generates metal. The original meaning of *metal* was ice and snow; the extended meaning came later.

*Chou* is frozen ground and gravel; *wei* is good farmland. Consider: does *chou* earth not generate wood? In the northeast, before the thaw, grass has already pushed up through the ground. Winter wheat in Hebei and Shandong survives in frozen soil — a good example. *Wei*, good farmland, generates every kind of wood. Put frozen ground, gravel or farmland into thin mud and the mud's function remains. Put them on ore and rock and plants still grow — the rock's function remains, and the function of *xu* earth is reinforced. But mix frozen ground, gravel and farmland together and all their functions weaken. That is why these two do not take each other into storage. Whether something goes into storage depends on whether the vault's function survives; if the vault's function is gone, both have become waste and cannot enter. By the same token, thin mud and ore have nothing to do with each other — add mud to ore in a smelter and no iron comes out. So *chen* and *xu* do not take each other. Entering storage is a form of cooperation: earth entering an earth vault is earth cooperating with earth. If two earths cannot cooperate and cannot profit each other, they cannot enter each other's storage. In practice this method of reading storage holds up; the traditional method does not. Wood enters the *wei* vault because *wei*'s function can still operate and the wood can profit. Metal is ice and snow, so it can enter *chou*, the vault of frozen ground and gravel. Water running into thin mud strengthens both — hence water enters the *chen* vault; a mudslide is the proof. Water flooding good farmland destroys the soil's function and the crops die. A mountain under permanent snow is like the metal-generating function of *xu* earth. Gravel and farmland both come from ore and rock, so *chou* and *wei* enter the *xu* vault. Fate prediction is prediction of the actual world, of every blade of grass and every tree in it. Abandon that kind of rational analysis of the actual world and every purely theoretical, taken-for-granted conclusion about storage is wrong.

## 3. The auspicious and inauspicious meaning of each mode of interaction

**In annual-cycle prediction, looking at the time system alone:**

**1. The useful god clashes the unfavourable god: the useful god's auspicious meaning appears, and the unfavourable god's auspicious meaning also appears. The unfavourable god clashes the useful god: the useful god shows its inauspicious meaning, and the unfavourable god also shows its inauspicious meaning.**

**2. The useful god combines with the unfavourable god: both the useful god's auspicious meaning and the unfavourable god's inauspicious meaning appear, but the auspicious predominates. The unfavourable god combines with the useful god: both appear, but the inauspicious predominates.**

**3. The useful god punishes or harms the unfavourable god: both meanings appear, half auspicious and half not.**

**4. The unfavourable god enters storage, and the vault has no relation to the day master: the unfavourable god shows an auspicious meaning.**

(An unfavourable god in the original chart can only enter a vault belonging to the annual cycle; without the annual cycle, the ten gods in the chart cannot enter storage. For example, *chou* and *wei* earth, and *wu* and *ji* earth, in the chart enter storage only in a *chen* year or a *xu* year. If an unfavourable earth in the chart enters the annual vault and the vault has no relation to the day master, that unfavourable god shows an auspicious meaning.)

**5. The useful god enters storage, and the vault has no relation to the day master: ordinary, a normal state.**

**6. The day master is in the vault: every ten god in the vault now has a relation with the day master inside it. The useful god shows its auspicious meaning, the unfavourable god its inauspicious meaning. If the ten gods in the vault have a generating relation among themselves, the final judgement follows the god at the endpoint.**

**7. Vault clashing vault: the active vault — including all the ten gods inside it — shows its full auspicious or inauspicious meaning. The passive vault, including all its ten gods, loses its meaning entirely.**

**8. When the passive vault is clashed, any ten god inside it that also appears in the chart shows its meaning too.**

**Note: generating and controlling apply only in relation to the day master. A useful god generating the day master is auspicious. A useful god controlling the day master means the officer-and-killer: seven auspicious, three inauspicious.**

(That is the entire secret and the entire set of judgements for reading by the time system. Understand the four vaults, know that twenty of the sixty stem-branch pairs are vault years, and you can read twenty years accurately by vault alone. Most inaccurate readings happen when a vault appears — traditional fate studies simply freeze when they meet one.)

**Whether the day master has a relation to the vault:**

**1. The day master sits on the vault, or the vault punishes, clashes, combines with or harms the day branch — then the day master has a relation to the vault.**

**2. What ten gods may be in the vault:**

**A. The stems and branches of the element taken into storage. In the *wei* vault, for instance, a quantity of wood.**

**B. Ten gods that have a punishing, clashing, combining or harming relation with the vault. A clash is generally negligible.**

Suppose the annual cycle is a *chen* year. If the chart contains *mao* wood (a harming relation), *shen* metal (combining), *you* metal (combining), *zi* water (combining, and also entering storage), *ren* water (entering storage), *gui* water (entering storage), *hai* water (entering storage), *chou* earth (entering storage), *wei* earth (entering storage), *wu* earth (entering storage) and *ji* earth (entering storage), all of these can enter the annual *chen* vault. If your day master happens to be *wu* or *ji* earth, or *ren* or *gui* water, then in the *chen* year you meet every ten god that has entered the vault: meet a useful god inside and something auspicious happens; meet an unfavourable god inside and something bad happens. Suppose the *mao* wood in the *chen* vault is a thug, the *shen* metal a villain, the *zi* water a bomb — go into that vault and you meet the thug's fists, the villain's extortion, the bomb's blast. Once we can read a bazi through its vaults, separating useful from unfavourable gods, the time system alone will read twenty of sixty years correctly. Traditional bazi does not know which ten god is active and which is passive, cannot separate the principal contradiction from the secondary one, and so its accuracy is very low. A clash may be treated as non-entry, but a clash still creates a relation — a clash collapses at a touch. The *chen* earth vault is a house; once it becomes a house its own meaning is largely lost, and a house has no auspicious or inauspicious meaning in itself. Grasp these eight rules and the time system alone will read eighty per cent of people correctly. For people with a common destiny it reaches one hundred per cent. For the exceptionally brilliant and the exceptionally unlucky it cannot — those require the bazi model and the three systems. In other words: by the time system alone, no theory whatsoever reaches one hundred per cent.

**Case one: male — Jiayin, Renshen, Jiachen, Xinwei**

**Luck cycle: Dingchou. Annual cycle: Wuxu.**

1. In the *wuxu* year, under the *dingchou* cycle, the earth wealth star is in chaotic motion.

2. *Xu* punishes *chou* and punishes *wei* — a punishing and also a vault relation, so in the *wuxu* year the earth does not fully enter storage. *Xu* is also the fire vault, taking *ding* fire into storage.

3. *Xu* clashes the day branch *chen* earth, and *xu* carries *ding* fire to the day branch: there is information of incoming money. *Xu* clashing *chen* earth sets it in chaotic motion, so the chaotic-motion information exists — but the energy of the motion is reduced. Wealth star in chaotic motion: money comes and money goes.

4. *Xu* punishes *wei*; *xin* metal, at the gate, is the useful god and is activated, standing for something auspicious. When the officer-and-killer is the useful god it is also, for a man, more auspicious than not. The auspicious content is career progress, but under pressure.

Fact: that year he did take in money, and his career did develop.

**Case two: male — Wuchen, Bingchen, Bingshen, Wuzi**

**Luck cycle: Jiwei. Annual cycle: Wuxu.**

1. In the *wuxu* year under the *jiwei* cycle, earth is in chaotic motion — the eating-and-wounding in chaotic motion. That means reckless investment and foolish decisions.

2. Interaction: the annual *wu* takes its position at the year stem and at the hour stem. *Xu* earth can take *jiwei* earth into storage, but this year the earth does not enter storage, and the *bing* fire day master also enters storage. The day master entering storage is inauspicious: some source of income will be lost. This year, do not invest recklessly; keep operations conservative.

**Case three: male — Bingyin, Gengzi, Renyin, Xinhai**

**Luck cycle: Guimao. Annual cycle: Wuxu.**

1. In the *wuxu* year under *guimao*, the parallel-and-rob water is in chaotic motion.

2. Interaction: the annual *wu* combines away the luck-cycle *gui*, reducing the water's chaotic motion; *xu* earth combines with the day-branch *yin* wood (officer-and-killer combining with eating-and-wounding is auspicious, since the officer-and-killer is the useful god). The officer-and-killer stands for benefactors, the eating-and-wounding for career. Meeting a benefactor's help develops one's career.

The annual *xu* earth takes *bing* fire into storage, and *xu*, carrying wealth, enters a relation with the day-branch *yin* wood: auspicious information of incoming money.

Actual feedback: career developed with a benefactor's help, and money came in.

**Case four: male — Gengxu, Bingxu, Xinsi, Jihai**

**Luck cycle: Xinmao. Annual cycle: Jiawu.**

1. In the *jiawu* year under *xinmao*, there is no chaotic motion.

2. Interaction: *jia* clashes *geng* (wealth clashing parallel), which is not close to the day master; *jia* combines with *ji* (wealth combining with seal) — the wealth star at the gate, auspicious information of incoming money. *Wu* combines with *xu*, not close to the day master, but it activates the *bing* fire useful god, the officer-and-killer — auspicious. The officer-and-killer as useful god stands for meeting benefactors and career development.

If this person had been born in the *wuxu* hour, the earth would be close to chaotic motion:

1. In the *jiawu* year under *xinmao*, earth is close to chaotic motion.

2. The *bing* fire useful god, officer-and-killer, combines with the body (six auspicious, four inauspicious) — and for a man the officer-and-killer is bad.

3. The annual *wu* fire combines with *xu*, and *wu* earth, the unfavourable god, arrives at the gate: inauspicious.

**Case five: male — Wushen, Jiazi, Yihai, Dingchou**

**Luck cycle: Jisi. Annual cycle: Wuxu.**

1. In the *wuxu* year under *jisi*, the earth wealth star is in chaotic motion (check whether the moving wealth star is close to the day master). In fact it is not close.

2. Interaction: *wu* takes its position at the year stem and *shen* metal is also activated; *shen* metal first combines with the month-branch *zi* water, and its relation with the day branch is slight and of little significance. So the auspicious meaning carried by *shen* metal, the officer-and-killer, is small, and the danger it carries is also small. Because the annual *xu* earth takes *ding* and *si* fire into storage, ideals are hard to realise.

3. This year's wealth star in chaotic motion has little to do with the day master, so the loss is benign.

Fact: he spent money building a house at home. Projects away from home could not be completed; ideals were hard to realise.

**Case six: male — Dingsi, Renzi, Renxu, Bingwu**

**Luck cycle: Wushen. Annual cycle: Wuxu.**

1. In the *wuxu* annual under *wushen*, the earth officer-and-killer is close to chaotic motion. The fire wealth star is close to chaotic motion.

2. Interaction: the annual *wu* takes its position at the luck-cycle stem, activating *shen* metal. The annual *xu* takes *ding, si, bing, wu* fire into storage and, carrying the wealth star, returns to the day branch — auspicious information, incoming money. So this year the wealth star's chaotic motion does him little harm.

Fact: his income exceeded his expenditure.

**Case seven: male — Jiayin, Dingchou, Jiaxu, Jiaxu (short in stature)**

**Luck cycles: Gengchen, Xinsi. Annual cycles: Bingxu, Dinghai, Wuzi.**

**First, the stature judgement.** In the original chart wood is the unfavourable god. There is the month-stem *ding* fire draining it, and the hour-stem *jia* wood supporting the body; on paper the day master is balanced. In fact he grew up short, which means the hour-stem *jia* wood, an unfavourable god, did not perform its unfavourable function — auspicious. And the month-stem *ding* fire, the useful god, did perform its useful function — also auspicious. So being short is one of the conditions of an upper-grade fate.

**Second, the time system.**

1. In the *bingxu* year under *gengxu*, the earth wealth star is in chaotic motion. The unfavourable wealth star is also closely related to the day master, which makes it that year's greatest danger. Fact: he invested and spent money.

2. In the *dinghai* year under *xinsi* there is no chaotic motion. *Ding* fire, the eating-and-wounding, at the gate: incoming money. *Hai* water, the useful god, combining with *yin*: also auspicious.

Fact: that year income was relatively large.

3. In the *wuzi* year under *xinsi*, *zi* combining with *chou* activates the month-branch *ding* fire, the eating-and-wounding, so there is still wealth information. But the energy is far smaller than in the *dinghai* year, because in *dinghai* the *ding* fire eating-and-wounding stood directly at the gate, and its activating energy was greater.

4. In the *jiawu* and *yiwei* years under *xinsi*: in *jiawu*, wood is the unfavourable god, but *wu* fire, the useful god, combines to the day master's side, and there is still wealth information. In *yiwei*, the hour wood, parallel-and-rob, is the unfavourable god, and the wealth star is also unfavourable: information of loss.

Fact: in those two years there were disputes, lawsuits and loss of money.
